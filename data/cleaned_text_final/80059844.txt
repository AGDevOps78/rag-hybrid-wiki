The impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on time perception, or the During the early months of the COVID-19 pandemic, people around the world reported that time felt "stretched", with days and weeks blending together, along with difficulty in recalling a sequence of recent events.   This phenomenon, sometimes called "temporal disintegration" in psychiatric literature, is characterized by impaired sequential thinking and a sense that the present is disconnected from the continuity of time and future.  People whose routines were the most disrupted were the most affected by distorted time perception.
