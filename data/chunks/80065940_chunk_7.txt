In addition to its redox properties, the polydopamine shell adsorbed flavins secreted by S. xiamenensis, enabling a double-mediator electron transport channel. The maximum power density was measured to be 452.8 mW/m2 for the nanoencapsulated S. xiamenensis (using a carbon felt electrode), 6.1 times higher than that of the naïve S. xiamenensis anode (74.7 mW/m2).
