FeS-functionalized S. oneidensis MR-1 exhibited better performance than polydopamine-functionalized S. oneidensis MR-1, reaching a maximum power density of 3.21 W/m2 (using the conventional carbon felt electrode)—a 14.7-fold increase over naïve S. oneidensis MR-1 (0.207 W/m2). polydopamine-functionalized S. oneidensis MR-1 achieved a maximum power density of 0.66 W/m2 under the same microbial fuel cell configuration.
