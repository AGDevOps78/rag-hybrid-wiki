Bronze was an extremely useful alloy to the ancients, because it is much stronger and harder than either of its components.[12] Steel was another common alloy. However, in ancient times, it could only be created as an accidental byproduct from the heating of iron ore in fires (smelting) during the manufacture of iron.[13] Other ancient alloys include pewter,[14] brass,[15] and pig iron.[16] In the modern age, steel can be created in many forms. Carbon steel can be made by varying only the carbon content, producing soft alloys like mild steel or hard alloys like spring steel. Alloy steels can be made by adding other elements, such as chromium, molybdenum, vanadium, or nickel, resulting in alloys such as high-speed steel or tool steel.
