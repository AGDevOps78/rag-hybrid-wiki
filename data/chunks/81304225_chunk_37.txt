Safety and scope-of-practice concerns are most salient where non-ordinary states or strong catharsis are involved. Scholarly and clinical discussions converge on screening for vulnerability; clear preparation and integration phases; monitoring for dissociation or destabilisation; explicit consent about symbolic versus doctrinal framings; and robust referral pathways when indicated. In symbolic counselling and oracular work, ethical guidance stresses non-directive framing and avoidance of deterministic claims.
