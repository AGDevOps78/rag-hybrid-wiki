Furthermore, Segura and Quiles also noted that the simultaneous chilling of the leaves and heating of the roots (whilst the plant is under illumination), can cause the slowing and eventual inhibition of PSII. This then led to an over-reduction in the PQ pool, which ultimately stimulated chlororespiration.
