also, it has been shown that five different toxins can be detected using five different cssncs simultaneously. in a move toward environmentally friendlier and less toxic cssncs, si quantum dots with various shells have been developed. si is 10 times safer than cd and current work is focused on making si more water-soluble and biocompatible. in particular, si quantum dots with poly (acrylic acid) and allylamine shells have been used in cell labeling. other in vitro uses include flow cyclometry, pathogen detection, and genomic and proteomic detection. because cssncs emit in the near-infrared region (700–900 nm) of the electromagnetic spectrum, imaging them is not complicated by autofluorescence of tissue, which occurs at higher frequencies (400–600 nm), and scattering effects. this has been used in the mapping of sentinel lymph-nodes in cancer surgery in animals. lymph nodes 1 cm deep were imaged and the excised nodes with cssnc accumulation were found to have the highest probability for containing metastatic cells.
