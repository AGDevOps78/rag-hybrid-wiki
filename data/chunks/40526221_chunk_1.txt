In Swedish, compounds like farhåga (lit. 'danger-håga'), räddhåga (lit. 'fear-håga'), mean "fear, concern" and thereof. Rather philosophical in description, hug, encompasses that in man that thinks, feels and wants; if the soul is understood as the seat of ideas, thoughts and memories, will and emotions. It can be specific emotions, moods, desires, wishes and the like. Compare the Swedish expressions sorgsen håg (lit. 'sad mind'), sorgsen i hågen (lit. 'sad in the mind').
