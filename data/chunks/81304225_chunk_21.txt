staged demonstrations and clinical case series helped normalise descriptive languages for trance phenomena and dissociation. in contemporary practice, indirect and narrative methods (often associated with ericksonian influence) treat images, metaphors and ritualised sequences as devices for re-patterning experience without realist–occult commitments.
