Thus, the term superelectrophilic anions was used. The superelectrophilic anions (B12X11)− can be generated from the very stable closo-dodecaborate dianions (B12X12)2− by fragmentation in the gas phase. The closo-dodecaborate dianions, which serve as precursors, consist of twelve boron atoms arranged in a highly symmetrical, cage-like closo structure.
