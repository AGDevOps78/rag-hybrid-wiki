These antiseptics help prevent infections by effectively eliminating UTI symptoms through their action on microorganisms. Nitrofurantoin is regarded as the first-line agent for simple cystitis, with an efficacy rate ranging from 88% to 92%. It can also be a prophylactic agent to prevent long-term UTIs. This antibacterial medication is effective against both gram-positive and gram-negative bacteria. Nitrofurantoin exhibits its bactericidal activity through various mechanisms, including inhibiting ribosomal translation, causing bacterial DNA damage and interfering with the citric acid cycle. However, the specific role of each mechanism remains to be further explored. When nitrofurantoin is metabolized, it converts into a reactive intermediate that attacks bacterial ribosomes, inhibiting bacterial protein synthesis. This medication is typically taken orally and has minimal systemic absorption, reducing potential side effects.
