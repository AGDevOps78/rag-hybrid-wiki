Macromolecular structures determined through X-ray crystallography (and other techniques) are housed in the Protein Data Bank (PDB)–a freely accessible repository for the structures of proteins and other biological macromolecules. There are many molecular graphics codes available for visualising these structures. The International Tables for Crystallography  is an eight-book series that outlines the standard notations for formatting, describing and testing crystals. The series contains books that covers analysis methods and the mathematical procedures for determining organic structure through x-ray crystallography, electron diffraction, and neutron diffraction.
