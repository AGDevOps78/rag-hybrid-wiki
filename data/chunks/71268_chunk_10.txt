A major focus of modern astrochemistry is the detection and formation of complex organic molecules. Observations have shown that cosmic dust contains amorphous organic solids with mixed aromatic–aliphatic structures,    and that polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) are widespread throughout the universe. PAHs may account for more than 20% of cosmic carbon and have been proposed as potential precursors to amino acids and nucleotides. Several notable detections have highlighted the astrochemical relevance of complex organics.
