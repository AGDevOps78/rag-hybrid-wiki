The HUN-REN Wigner Research Centre for Physics is the largest Hungarian research institute studying physics. Formerly a research institute of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences, it became a member of the Eötvös Loránd Research Network and after the ELKH's reorganisation it became part of the HUN-REN Hungarian Research Network. The Institute of Particle and Nuclear Physics conducts successful experimental and theoretical exploratory research in the fields of particle physics, nuclear physics, gravitational research, space physics, nuclear solid-state physics and materials science, as well as computational sciences. [citation needed] The Institute of Solid State Physics and Optics is engaged in research and development of atomic-level material structure, quantum physics, quantum optics, statistical physics and laser applications.
