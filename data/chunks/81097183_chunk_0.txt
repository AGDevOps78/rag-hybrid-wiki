King Kong Balls (popularly known as Monkey Balls) is a giant Czech statue created by Denis Defrancesco originally placed in Prague but later moved to Beroun, depicting a blue monkey with a large golden scrotum. The statue was created after Defrancesco saw an image of a real monkey lounging with large testicles.
