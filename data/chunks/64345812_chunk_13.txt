Matrix content ranging from 10 to 40 wt% can shift the mechanical properties of the electrolyte from a soft paste into a hard gel. However, a tradeoff between mechanical strength and ionic conductivity as one goes up with changing matrix content the other suffers. Despite this, matrix content in these materials can have added benefits including enhanced lithium transference number due to functionalized matrix materials. These new classes of QSSEs are an active area of research to develop the optimal combination of matrix and solvent.
