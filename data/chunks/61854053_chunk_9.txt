MACE, in principle allows the fabrication of steep trenches but is still cheap compared to gas-phase etching methods. Metal assisted chemical etching allows for the production of porous silicon with photoluminescence. Black silicon is silicon with a modified surface and is a type of porous silicon. There are several works[clarification needed] on obtaining black silicon using MACE technology. The main application of black silicon is solar energy. Black gallium arsenide with light trapping properties have been also produced by MACE.
