Traditional ISEs are typically single-phase materials. Oxide-based conductors are widely studied for their excellent chemical stability and mechanical robustness[27]. Within oxide ISEs there are many crystalline structure types, include LISICON (lithium superionic conductor) (e.g. LGPS, LiSiPS, LiPS), argyrodite-like (e.g. Li6PS5X, X = Cl, Br, I),[28] garnets (LLZO),[29] NASICON (sodium superionic conductor) (e.g. LTP, LATP, LAGP),[30] lithium nitrides (e.g. Li3N),[31] lithium hydrides (LiBH4),[32] lithium phosphidotrielates [33] and phoshidotetrelates,[34] perovskites (e.g. lithium lanthanum titanate, "LLTO")[35].
