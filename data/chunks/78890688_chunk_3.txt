Many musicians are not aware of the health risks that come with being a musician. According to the German Occupational Safety Law relating to Noise and Vibration (Lärm- und Vibrations-Arbeitsschutzverordnung) employees have to be protected from damaging noise. Many employers often are not aware how demanding the job really is, since it includes many different tasks, such as teaching music or a lot of practice. For these instances, Germany set up a training programme to help identify how affected a musician is by being exposed to loud sounds and what preventative measures should be established.
