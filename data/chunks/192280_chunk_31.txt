A stereoscope is a tool to be able to offer the two images of a stereogram separately and sharply to both eyes and at a different distance than where the eyes converge. Different stereoscopes are suitable for different types of stereograms. There are different types of stereoscopes, based on lenses, mirrors, prisms, color filters and polaroid filters. The first stereoscope was invented by Wheatstone in Greater depth experience. To increase the sense of depth, the left and right images of stereo photographs are sometimes placed slightly further apart than they should be for a realistic image. The explanation for the greater sense of depth at a greater convergence distance is that the eyes have to look slightly farther away (converge) than is appropriate for the original situation and the monocular perspective in the images.
