Additional product standards identify preferred thread sizes for screws and nuts, as well as corresponding bolt head and nut sizes, to facilitate compatibility between spanners (wrenches) and other tools. The most common threads in use are the ISO metric screw threads (M) for most purposes, and BSP threads (R, G) for pipes.
