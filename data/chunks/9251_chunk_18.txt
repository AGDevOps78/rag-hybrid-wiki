His slogan, salus populi suprema lex esto, translates as "the health (or safety, or welfare) of the people shall be the supreme law."  The study of failed products is known as forensic engineering. It attempts to identify the cause of failure to allow a redesign of the product and so prevent a re-occurrence. Careful analysis is needed to establish the cause of failure of a product. The consequences of a failure may vary in severity from the minor cost of a machine breakdown to large loss of life in the case of accidents involving aircraft and large stationary structures like buildings and dams. These larger scale engineering disasters can arise from shortcuts or errors in the design process, such as miscalculations and miscommunication. They can also happen as a result of fatigue failure due to stress, temperature, or corrosion. Faulty computer software can also play a role.
