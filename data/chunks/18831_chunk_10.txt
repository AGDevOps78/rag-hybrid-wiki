discrete geometry, the study of finite configurations in geometry. convex geometry, the study of convex sets, which takes its importance from its applications in optimization. complex geometry, the geometry obtained by replacing real numbers with complex numbers. === algebra === algebra is the art of manipulating equations and formulas. diophantus (3rd century) and al-khwarizmi (9th century) were the two main precursors of algebra. diophantus solved some equations involving unknown natural numbers by deducing new relations until he obtained the solution. al-khwarizmi introduced systematic methods for transforming equations, such as moving a term from one side of an equation into the other side. the term algebra is derived from the arabic word al-jabr meaning 'the reunion of broken parts' that he used for naming one of these methods in the title of his main treatise.
