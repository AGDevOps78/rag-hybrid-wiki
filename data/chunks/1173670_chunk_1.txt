The fellowship, and the specialty, are heavily research-oriented and academically based, although less exclusively now than in past decades. The principal North American professional association was originally named the Lawson Wilkins Pediatric Endocrine Society,[3] now renamed the Pediatric Endocrine Society. Other longstanding pediatric endocrine associations include the European Society for Paediatric Endocrinology, the British Society for Paediatric Endocrinology, the Australasian Paediatric Endocrine Group and the Japanese Society for Pediatric Endocrinology.
