Computer science plays a very important role in modern sports. It is used to analyze players’ performance, fitness, and health with the help of computers and software. Coaches use data and video analysis to plan better strategies and improve team performance. Technologies like sensors, cameras, and wearable devices help in training and injury prevention. Computers are also used for fair decision-making through systems like VAR and digital scoreboards. Online platforms and live streaming allow people to watch sports from anywhere in the world. Overall, computer science has made sports more advanced, accurate, and efficient. Computer science in sport is an interdisciplinary discipline that has its goal in combining the theoretical as well as practical aspects and methods of the areas of informatics and sport science.
