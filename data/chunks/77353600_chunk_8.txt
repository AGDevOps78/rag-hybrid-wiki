LAMA2-MD is caused by pathogenic variants or mutations in the LAMA2 gene that encodes alpha2 chain of laminin-211 or laminin-alpha2, previously known as laminin type 2 or merosin. laminin-211 is important to the function and integrity of the sarcolemma of muscle fibers. laminin-alpha2 is also present in extra-muscular locations as the central and peripheral nervous system.
