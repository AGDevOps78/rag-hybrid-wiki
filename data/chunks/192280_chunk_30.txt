A common way of doing this is with a stereogram in which the image for the left eye is on the right and the image for the right eye is on the left (R-L stereogram). The practice now is to cross the eye axes at a point in front of the stereogram in such a way that the left eye looks at the center of the right image and the right eye looks at the center of the left image.
