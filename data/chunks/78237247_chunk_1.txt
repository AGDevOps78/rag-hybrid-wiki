Numerous chromatin regulators also contain PEs, though these are less conserved than PEs within RBPs such as the SR proteins. Multiple spliceosomal components contain PEs. Certain PEs may occur only in specific tissues. PE-containing transcripts generally represent a minority of the overall transcript population, in part due to their active degradation via NMD, though this relative abundance can be elevated upon inhibition of NMD or certain biological states. Certain PE-containing transcripts are resistant to NMD and may be translated into truncated proteins. Cis-regulatory elements neighboring PEs have been found to affect PE inclusion. Many proteins whose corresponding genes contain PEs autoregulate PE inclusion in their respective transcripts and thereby control their own levels via a feedback loop. Cross-regulation of PE inclusion has also been observed.
