During the flight, photographs of the terrain are taken at regular time intervals. Consecutive photos are viewed in pairs in a stereoscope. Camouflaged objects can now be clearly seen in depth. This stereogram illustrates that a stereogram can also be made with one camera, and that binocular depth perception helps to see through camouflage.
