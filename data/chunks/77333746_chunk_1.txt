Neurodevelopmental disorder with hypotonia, brain anomalies, distinctive facies, and absent language (NEDHAFA) was a suggested name for this syndrome, however, ReNU syndrome (pronounced 'renew') was chosen through a collaboration between researchers and the families of those impacted by variants in RNU4-2. The name symbolises that this diagnosis “renews” hope for a brighter future for all those affected. An etiologically related disorder RNU2-2 syndrome has been identified by Greene and Turro. RNU2-2 syndrome is a major spliceosome disorder with similar symptoms to RNU4-2 / ReNU syndrome but it is five times less prevalent.
