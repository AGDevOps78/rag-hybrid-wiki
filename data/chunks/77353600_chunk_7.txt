Respiratory insufficiency can occur in both types of LAMA2-MD. Respiratory tract infections are a cause of death in the congenital type 1A or MDC1A. Cardiac involvement in LAMA2-MD may manifest in dilated cardiomyopathy and systolic dysfunction. Cardiac screening and surveillance are important in LAMA2-MD. This is aimed at timely diagnosis and management of subclinical cardiac involvement. Epilepsy is a fairly common manifestation of both types of LAMA2-MD. However, the age at occurrence of first epileptic fit is earlier in the congenital type 1A or MDC1A. Screening for epilepsy should be included in the workup.
