According to the company, the development of the QpiAI-Indus quantum computer will support deep-tech innovation across various fields of life sciences, drug discovery, materials science, mobility, climate action, and sustainability. The history of the development of the QpiAI-Indus quantum computer can be traced from the foundation of the company QpiAI in the year The primary hardware components of the QpiAI-Indus quantum computer are Quantum Processing Unit (QPU), Cryogenic System, Quantum Control and Readout Electronics (QpiAISenseTM) and Classical High-Performance Computing (HPC) Integration. The quantum processing unit (QPU) consists of qubits technology and noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) device. The qubits technology in the processor is based on superconducting qubits, specifically Transmon qubits, which is a type of superconducting circuit used for quantum computation. According to the founder cum CEO Dr Nagendra Nagaraja, the T1 and T2 times for the current 25-qubits quantum computer QpiAI-Indus are 30 μs and 25 μs, respectively. These are characterized by the in-house developed quantum control electronics cluster in the company at Bangalore. They are expected to increase to 100 μs soon.
