Activists for education and the war on drugs encourage youth sport as a means to increase educational participation and to fight the illegal drug trade. According to the Center for Injury Research and Policy at Nationwide Children's Hospital, the biggest risk for youth sport is death or serious injury including concussion. These risks come from running, basketball, association football, volleyball, gridiron, gymnastics, and ice hockey.[46] Youth sport in the US is a $15 billion industry including equipment up to private coaching.[47] Disabled or adaptive sports are played by people with a disability, including physical and intellectual disabilities.[48] As many of these are based on existing sports modified to meet the needs of people with a disability, they are sometimes referred to as adapted sports.[49] However, not all disabled sports are adapted; several that have been specifically created for people with a disability have no equivalent in able-bodied sports, such as goalball and boccia.[50] Masters sport, senior sport, or veteran sport is an age category of sport, that usually contains age groups of those 35 and older.[51] It may concern unaltered or adapted sport activities, with and without competitions.
