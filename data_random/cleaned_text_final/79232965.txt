Incompatibility rule (also incompatibility of membership) is a regulation issued by a group of people (a government, political party, or other association) that prohibits simultaneous membership in this group and other groups (explicitly listed). Such conflict usually represents an obstacle to admission to the group or a cause for expulsion.
