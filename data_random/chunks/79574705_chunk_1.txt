Unlike multi-system disorders like Cornelia de Lange syndrome, LINKED syndrome affects both neurological structures and craniofacial regions exclusively. The primary cause of LINKED syndrome is mutations in the OTUD5 gene, which encodes a deubiquitinating enzyme essential for regulating protein stability during embryogenesis, named Otu Deubiquitinase 5.
