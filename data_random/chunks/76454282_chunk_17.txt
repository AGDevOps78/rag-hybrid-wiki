FDA approval covers various mini probes for specific anatomical indications, and recent updates have extended approval to new models and the use of additional contrast agents. Additionally, in Europe, CLE devices (including the Cellvizio system and related miniprobes) are regulated as Class IIa medical devices and must bear CE marking to be distributed and used clinically.
