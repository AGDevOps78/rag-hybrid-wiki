As CLE often encounters image distortions, the degradation of image quality and loss of image information, eventually increase the difficulty of accurate diagnosis. A new image quality assessment (IQA) utilising Weber's Law and local descriptors assesses the quality and filters images with low diagnostic value. The variety of pathology conditions identifiable by CLE is limited. The histological diagnosis is limited to cancerous lesions and inflammation where the number of specific diseases identifiable is not numerous. Moreover, it requires specific training to operate CLE and correctly interpret CLE images, which are rarely used skills by experts in endoscopy. Owing to the narrow field of view, the applications of CLE might be restricted. Computer-aided diagnosis with AI technology may be beneficial in diagnosing CLE images.
