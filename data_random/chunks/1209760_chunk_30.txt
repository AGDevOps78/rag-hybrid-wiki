The N-type calcium channel blocker ziconotide is an analgesic based on a cyclic peptide cone snail toxin (ω-conotoxin MVIIA) from the species Conus magus. Numerous anti-infectives are based on natural products. The first antibiotic to be discovered, penicillin, was isolated from the mold Penicillium. Penicillin and related beta lactams work by inhibiting the DD-transpeptidase enzyme that is required by bacteria to cross link peptidoglycan to form the cell wall. Several natural product drugs target tubulin, which is a component of the cytoskeleton. These include the tubulin polymerization inhibitor colchicine isolated from the Colchicum autumnale (autumn crocus flowering plant), which is used to treat gout. Colchicine is biosynthesized from the amino acids phenylalanine and tryptophan. Paclitaxel, in contrast, is a tubulin polymerization stabilizer and is used as a chemotherapeutic drug.
