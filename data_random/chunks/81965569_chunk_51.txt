For order 8 square, we just begin directly with the six cells. Singly even order: For singly even order, we have the algorithm given by al-Antaki. Here the corner cells are occupied by n and n − 1. Below is an example of 10th-order square. Start by placing 1 at the bottom row next to the left corner cell, then place 2 in the top row. After this, place 3 at the bottom row and turn around the border in anti-clockwise direction placing the next numbers, until n − 2 is reached on the right column. The next two numbers are placed in the upper corners (n − 1 in upper left corner and n in upper right corner). Then, the next two numbers are placed on the left column, then we resume the cyclic placement of the numbers until half of all the border cells are filled.
