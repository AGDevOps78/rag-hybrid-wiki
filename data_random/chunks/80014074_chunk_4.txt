The symbols show the perceived depth. At small inclinations (rotations) the perceived images are seen at the positions of the ghost images C, D. From Panum's limiting case the images are seen at the positions of the real images A, B. The latter can be explained by the fact that C, D are then outside the area for stereopsis. Conclusion: at small inclinations the binocular ghost images (C, D) are indeed seen. At inclinations greater than Panum's limiting case the real objects (A, B) are seen. The hypothesis that binocular ghost images are seen in the basic double-nail illusion leads to the prediction of different appearances when the fixation point and the distance, shape or orientation of the two "nails" are varied.
