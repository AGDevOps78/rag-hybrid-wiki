Parkyn used McIntosh electro-thermal cabinets equipped with colored incandescent bulbs, applying different hues according to their psychological effects. Red was used to stimulate, blue to calm, and green to reduce activity.[62][57][58][63] The McIntosh Battery and Optical Company, located in Chicago, made the latest equipment for Electro-Therapeutics, X-Rays, and other experimental work. Parkyn used these devices in his research and they were heavily advertised in his magazines.[65] Dr. Parkyn's clinical work at the Chicago School of Psychology was closely associated with the use of the Allison physician's table and chair, manufactured by the W.
