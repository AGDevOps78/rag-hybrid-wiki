In October Dolma or tolma is claimed by both Armenia and Azerbaijan. Armenia holds an annual tolma festival, always at a site that has historical significance in its conflicts with Azerbaijan. Both Nicaragua and Costa Rica claim gallo pinto as their own, and the dish's origin is a point of contention between the two countries. The competition between the two countries over ownership of the dish is sometimes referred to as the "Gallo Pinto War". Falafel is argued over by Israel and various Arab states; according to Jennie Ebeling, writing in the Review of Middle East Studies, the dish "is loaded with issues of national identity". According to Alexander Lee, writing for History Today in The dish features prominently in Israeli cuisine and has been called a national dish. Some Palestinians and other Arabs have objected to the identification of falafel with Israeli cuisine as amounting to cultural appropriation.
