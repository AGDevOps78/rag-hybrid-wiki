Rotary kiln processes, where the ore is mixed with coal at high temperature, constitute the second category. These processes consist of an annular furnace in which iron ore mixed with coal is circulated. Hot reducing gases flow over, and sometimes through, the charge. The ore is deposited on a tray, or carts, rotating slowly in the furnace. After one rotation, the ore is reduced; it is then discharged and replaced by oxidized ore. A number of processes have been developed based on this principle.
