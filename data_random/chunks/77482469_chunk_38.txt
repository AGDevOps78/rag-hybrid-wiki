Elderly intersex people can feel concerned about home care as well; they may fear caretaker abuse due to their intersex variation or surgical changes to their body. Healthcare for intersex people in developing countries can be especially difficult to access due to cost or low availability. For instance, Vietnam only has one pediatric endocrinology department in the country, the National Hospital in Hanoi. Due to the lack of government funding towards healthcare, the cost of basic medications like estrogen tablets can be a major financial burden. In Bangladesh, the lack of treatment from government healthcare facilities can pose issues for intersex people if they cannot afford private care or do not have a private health facility near them.
