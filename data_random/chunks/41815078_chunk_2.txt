The latest Yahoo transparency report was released on 25 September There is much debate surrounding the efficacy of transparency reporting. Some critics argue that the optional nature of mere quantities of requests may mislead consumers, since most entities have little control over the number of requests they receive, the breadth of the requests they receive, or even the number of requests they ultimately comply with. Transparency reports play a vital role in promoting greater accountability and openness, but they are not immune to data discrepancies.
