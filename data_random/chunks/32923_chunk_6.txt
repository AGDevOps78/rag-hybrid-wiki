In the 18th century Metaphysical poetry fell into further disrepute,[26] while the interest in Elizabethan poetry was rekindled through the scholarship of Thomas Warton and others. This reassessment has resulted in a whole scale expansion of what is considered "literature", and genres hitherto not regarded as "literary", such as children's writing, journals, letters, travel writing, and many others are now the subjects of scholarly interest.[32][33][34] The Western literary canon has also expanded to include the literature of Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and South America.
