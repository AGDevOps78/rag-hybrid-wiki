Diabetes patients with peripheral neuropathy and peripheral artery disease are at risk of developing foot ulcers and infection. Poor knowledge about self-care increases the risk of amputation. Adoption of suitable preventative measures and early treatment of diabetic foot problems are important components of diabetes foot care. Good knowledge and practice regarding diabetes foot care can reduce the risk of foot complications and amputation. Regular examination of the foot is one of the fundamental steps to modifying the foot risk factors thereby reducing the risk of ulceration and amputation. Footwear tailored to the specific pathology of the patient can enable conservative management of the foot including debridement of the callus. Appropriate footwear can reduce abnormal pressure, reduce the rate of formation callus and ulcers and protect the foot from external trauma. Regular monitoring of blood glucose and optimal glucose control is a major part of diabetes self-management.
