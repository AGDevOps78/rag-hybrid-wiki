For instance, Planned Parenthood describes being intersex as a "naturally occurring variation" rather than a medical problem. Professor Elizabeth Reis supports changing disorders of sex development to divergence of sex development, writing that "using divergence, intersex people would not be labeled as being in a physical state absolutely in need of repair."  DSD is sometimes used to abbreviate differences of sex development rather than disorders of sex development. Some parents of intersex children prefer the term "disorders of sex development" because the term intersex evokes ideas of sexuality or that their child is a third gender rather than a boy or girl.
