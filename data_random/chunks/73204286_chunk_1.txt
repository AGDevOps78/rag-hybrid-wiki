The total carbon footprint of the internet, our electronic devices, and supporting elements add up to about 3.7% of global greenhouse gas emissions. It is as much as for the airline industry and the number keeps on rising.[7] This increase in energy consumption has a negative impact on the environment, as it contributes to climate change and air pollution.
