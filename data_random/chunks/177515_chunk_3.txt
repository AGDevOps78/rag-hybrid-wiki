Some applications of technologies where molecular engineering plays a critical role: Molecular engineers utilize sophisticated tools and instruments to make and analyze the interactions of molecules and the surfaces of materials at the molecular and nano-scale. The complexity of molecules being introduced at the surface is increasing, and the techniques used to analyze surface characteristics at the molecular level are ever-changing and improving. Meantime, advancements in high performance computing have greatly expanded the use of computer simulation in the study of molecular scale systems. At least three universities offer graduate degrees dedicated to molecular engineering: the University of Chicago,  the University of Washington,  and Kyoto University. These programs are interdisciplinary institutes with faculty from several research areas. The academic journal Molecular Systems Design & Engineering  publishes research from a wide variety of subject areas that demonstrates "a molecular design or optimisation strategy targeting specific systems functionality and performance."
