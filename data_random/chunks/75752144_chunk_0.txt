The technosphere can be considered one of the spheres of the Earth, like the lithosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, pedosphere and biosphere. It includes all non-living human creations, such as buildings, roads, vehicles, power plants, electricity grids, agricultural machinery, books and computers. However, whereas the Anthroposphere is used more broadly to include humans, livestock and managed ecosystems, and the Noosphere more narrowly focused on human rationality, the Technosphere specifically evokes the physical constructions created by humanity.[3][4] It has been argued that there has been insufficient research on the technosphere, and considerably more study is required to understand how it works as a global system.[5] The Technosphere has grown rapidly since the early 20th century.
