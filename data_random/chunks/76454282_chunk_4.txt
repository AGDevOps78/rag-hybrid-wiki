CLE reveals "soccer ball-like pattern" of narrower capillaries in malignant lymphomas; distorted architecture and fluorescein leakage from lumen in colonic adenocarcinoma; and blunt-shaped villi and crypts and increased intraepithelial lymphocytes in coeliac disease. CLE can be utilized to identify adenoma and neoplasia in colorectal polyps and lesions. CLE can be used for the identification of IBD and its subtypes (Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis) based on the observation on morphological characteristics, such as architectural distortion, lowered crypt density, crypt irregularity and an abnormally high density of epithelial gaps.
