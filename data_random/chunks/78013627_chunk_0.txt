Drone Emergency Medical Services (DEMS) involve the use of highly autonomous Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) drones to deliver critical medical supplies, such as Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs), life-saving medications, and remote diagnostic equipment, directly to emergency situations. This innovative approach is gaining traction globally, as it significantly reduces response times, thereby improving patient outcomes in time-sensitive scenarios like cardiac arrests and other emergencies where every second counts. The evolution of drone technology in EMS has been fueled by advancements in unmanned aerial systems (UAS) and the growing recognition of their capabilities in addressing logistical challenges, particularly in remote or underserved areas.
