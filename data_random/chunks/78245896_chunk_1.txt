Other operations need continuous medium or high flow rates.[3] The American Petroleum Institute (API) has published "API 17H recommended practice for subsea production systems", which provides specifications for standard types of hydraulic hot stabs with flow path bores ranging from 0.25 to 3.5 inches (6.4 to 88.9 mm).[2] A hot stab can be specified by the inside diameter of the receptacle, the bore and number of the ports and the type of handle. API/ISO specifications identify stabs by type, size, and port geometry and are interchangeable if the API/ISO-type, size and port specification match.[3] Removal of the plug stab (also known as the dummy or parking stab) from the receptacle is followed by insertion of the hot stab into the port. This operation can be done by a diver or a ROV with manipulator arm. The stab is not a self-locking mechanism, and though the internal forces may be balanced, an external force is required to secure the stab in place in the receptacle.[1]
