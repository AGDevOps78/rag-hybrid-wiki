The McIntosh Battery and Optical Company, located in Chicago, made the latest equipment for Electro-Therapeutics, X-Rays, and other experimental work. Parkyn used these devices in his research and they were heavily advertised in his magazines. Dr. Parkyn's clinical work at the Chicago School of Psychology was closely associated with the use of the Allison physician's table and chair, manufactured by the W. D. Allison Company of Indianapolis. Parkyn used the adjustable Allison table in his daily practice, reclining patients during suggestive treatment sessions to improve circulation and induce relaxation.
