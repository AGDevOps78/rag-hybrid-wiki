Lisa Lerer is an American journalist who is a national political correspondent for The New York Times. She is the co-author, with Elizabeth Dias, of The Fall of Roe: The Rise of a New America. Lerer covered national politics for nearly two decades, including five presidential campaigns, the White House, Congress and congressional races.
