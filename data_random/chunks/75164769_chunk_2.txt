Regular monitoring of blood glucose and optimal glucose control is a major part of diabetes self-management. Diabetes patients need to be capable of testing blood sugar at home at the recommended frequency. Frequent self- monitoring of blood glucose and record keeping is key to identifying the possibility of hypoglycemia. Diabetes patients should be able to know how to respond when blood sugar levels are too high or too low. Effective medication is the cornerstone of the proper treatment of diseases. Many patients fail to take the medication as prescribed and many patients prematurely discontinue their medication. Poor medication adherence in patients with diabetes is a costly public health challenge in many healthcare systems.
