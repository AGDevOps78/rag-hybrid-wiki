When the surfaces are not in contact, the lever signal will be denoted as S0. This value corresponds to the non-deformed lever. In the constant compliance region, the lever signal is simply a linear function of the displacement, and can be represented as a straight line The parameters a and b can be obtained from a least-squares fit of the constant compliance region. The inverse slope a−1 is also referred to as the optical lever sensitivity. By inverting this relation for the lever signal S0, which corresponds to the non-deformed lever, one can accurately obtain the contact point from D0 = (S0 − b)/a. Depending on the substrate, the precision in determining this contact point is between 0.5–2 nm.
