This often intersects with health and illness, but also theories of bodies as political, social, cultural, economic and ideological productions.[149] The ISA maintains a Research Committee devoted to "the Body in the Social Sciences".[150] A subfield of the sociology of health and illness that overlaps with cultural sociology is the study of death, dying and bereavement,[151] sometimes referred to broadly as the sociology of death.
