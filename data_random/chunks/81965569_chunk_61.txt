Once a 3×3 Medjig square has been constructed, it can be converted into a 6×6 magic square. For example, using the left most 3×3 Medjig square given above: There are 1,740,800 such 3×3 Medjig squares. An easy approach to construct higher order odd Medjig square is by wrapping a smaller odd ordered Medjig square with a Medjig border, just as with even ordered Medjig squares. Another approach is to append a row and a column to an even ordered Medjig square. Approaches such as the LUX method can also be used.
