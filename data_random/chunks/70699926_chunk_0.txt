The World Engineering Day for Sustainable Development (acronym: WED) is one of the UNESCO international days and is celebrated every 4 March. It was proclaimed by UNESCO General Conference on 25 November   It is celebrated by major engineering professional institutions, such as the Institution of Civil Engineers,  the China Association for Science and Technology,  Engineers Canada,  Engineers Australia,  Ingénieurs et scientifiques de France,  as well as NGOs such as DiscoverE,  Engineers Without Borders,  universities such as Imperial College London  or Technische Hochschule Georg Agricola  and corporations supporting the event.
