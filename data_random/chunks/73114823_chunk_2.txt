Examples of such cameras are backup cameras, surround view cameras, blind spot monitor cameras, forward-looking cameras and driver monitoring cameras. The GMSL link provides camera power, camera control, fault reporting and video transport across a single, coaxial cable. ADAS systems are considered safety-critical systems, and typically have an Automotive Safety Integrity Level (ASIL) rating, specified according to the ISO There are other uses for GMSL in the vehicle. Examples include control data distribution for adaptive driving beam headlights,  transport of radar and lidar data in autonomous vehicles, and video data transport between domain controllers in a vehicle. For example, surround view camera data can be transported from the ADAS domain controller to the head unit to be displayed to the driver. Automotive electronics must operate in an environment with extreme temperatures, high levels of electrical crosstalk and transients.
