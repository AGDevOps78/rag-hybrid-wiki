According to Tennov, Don Juan was probably nonlimerent, "more interested in exploiting the feeling in others for his own sexual gratification", although nonlimerence doesn't necessitate this. "Love addiction" is a heterogeneous construct under discussion as a potential mental disorder, but does not yet exist in any psychiatric nosology (e.g. not in the DSM). Academics do not currently agree on when love is an addiction or when it needs to be treated. In a narrow view, love could be considered addiction only when it involves abnormal processes carrying negative consequences; alternatively, a broader view is that all love might be addiction, or simply an appetite, similar to how humans are dependent on food. Authors such as Helen Fisher have also included those "who have been rejected or broken up with" as love addicts.
