In Biradicals are molecules bearing two unpaired electrons. These radicals can interact ferromagnetically (triplet), antiferromagnetically (open-shell singlet) or not interact at all (two-doublet). Biradicaloids/diradicaloids are a class of biradicals with significant radical centre interaction. The first phosphorus biradical was reported in Villinger et al later synthesised a stable cyclopentane-1,3-diyl biradical by the insertion of CO into a P–N bond of diphosphadiazanediyl. In The species by Villinger can undergo reaction with phosphaalkyne forming a five-membered P2N2C heterocycle with a P-C bridge. It can also undergo halogenation and reaction with elemental sulfur.
