The report says that the data indicates women tend to hold about 30% of the representation in public administration, education, and the environment, but men continue to dominate crucial policy areas like economics, defence, justice, and home affairs portfolios. Female cabinet ministers constitute 12% in defence and local government portfolios, 11% in energy, natural resource fuels, and mining portfolios, and 8% in transport portfolio. According to UN Women Alexandra Topping of The Guardian references a report on women's leadership within the multilateral system, which highlights the persistent gender power gap that has existed since the establishment of the United Nations.
