Virgil Neal, who also performed on stage under the name Xenophon LaMotte Sage, would later be involved with many New Thought institution, including the infamous New York Institute of Science, before becoming a massively successful manufacturer of cosmetics based in France, where he would build the Château d’Azur in the hills above Nice. Several prominent African American figures in medicine and civil rights were graduates of The Chicago School of Psychology, including Dr. Charles Edwin Bentley and Dr. J. B. Oliver.
