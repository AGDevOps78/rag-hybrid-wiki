This includes studying many different aspects of society, for example demographic trends,[166] political or technological trends,[167] or changes in culture. Within this field, sociologists often use macrosociological methods or historical-comparative methods. In contemporary studies of social change, there are overlaps with international development or community development. However, most of the founders of sociology had theories of social change based on their study of history.
