EFPI allows therapists and practitioners to test the beliefs of those with depression or anxiety and to potentially correct any instances of cognitive immunization. Cognitive immunization is considered a maladaptive cognitive process that rejects or dismisses expectation-disconfirming information. This phenomenon can be related to the violEx model,  which examines how individuals adjust their expectations in light of new evidence. According to this model, when an individual rejects novel information in favor of maintaining outdated or biased beliefs, they are cognitively immunizing themselves against disconfirming information.
