People from economically disadvantaged backgrounds can have limited access to care which is one reason for inadequate coordination between healthcare providers and patients. Healthcare professionals play a crucial role in diabetes self-management. Diabetes patients rely on health care professionals to obtain information and support in developing an individualised self-management plan. Self-management goals and recommendations given by health care professionals were identified to have a significant positive impact on the eating habits and physical activity of diabetes patients. Lack of proper emphasis on self-management may suggest to diabetes patients that self-management is either less important or even unimportant for diabetes management.
