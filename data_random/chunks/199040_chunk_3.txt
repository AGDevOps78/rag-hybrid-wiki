Specific conditions of the temperature and pressure, as well as the presence of catalysts, may be indicated in the same way. This is very useful in illustrating multi-step reaction mechanisms. Note that the substances above or below the arrows are not catalysts in this case, because they are consumed or produced in the reaction like ordinary reactants or products.
