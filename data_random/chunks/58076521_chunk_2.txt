Of interest for upgrading methane is its oxidative coupling: Conversion of carbon dioxide to unsaturated hydrocarbons via electrochemical reduction is a hopeful avenue of research. Still, no stable and economic technology has yet been developed. Methane, carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide, and methanol are substrates and products of enzymatic processes. In methanogenesis, carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide, and methanol are converted to methane. Methanogenesis by methanogenic archaea is reversible. In photosynthesis, carbon dioxide and water are converted to sugars (and O2), the energy for this (thermally) uphill reaction being provided by sunlight.
