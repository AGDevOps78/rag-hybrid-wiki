Methane, carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide, and methanol are substrates and products of enzymatic processes.[3] In methanogenesis, carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide, and methanol are converted to methane.[4] Methanogenesis by methanogenic archaea is reversible.[5] In photosynthesis, carbon dioxide and water are converted to sugars (and O2), the energy for this (thermally) uphill reaction being provided by sunlight.
