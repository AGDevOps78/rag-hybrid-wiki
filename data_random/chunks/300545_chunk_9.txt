According to Esperanza Herrero, women played a significant role in developing the Two-Step Flow theory. A woman named Joan Doris Goldhamer testified that she vividly remembers how she and several other women were calculating percentages, collecting data, and handling technical work. They also conducted many detailed and well-structured interviews and assisted in interpreting the information they helped gather for the theory.
