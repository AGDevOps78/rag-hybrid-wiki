On April 11, In one of the most widely publicized examinations of psychic claims in America, Dr. Parkyn entered into a public telepathy duel with Miss Maud Lancaster of London, known in the press as the "Lady Sherlock Holmes." Lancaster claimed she had assisted Scotland Yard with her psychic abilities and was touring the United States demonstrating what she called genuine telepathy. After Dr. Parkyn and William Walker Atkinson attended her exhibition at the Chicago Press Club on January 24, The duel was held on February 12, The event received extensive press coverage and became a landmark example of a public psychic claim tested under controlled observation. Although Lancaster continued her tour, the Chicago duel marked a turning point in how her abilities were regarded. Parkyn was praised for demanding proper test procedures and showing that dramatic stage results were not evidence of telepathy. He and Atkinson nonetheless acknowledged Lancaster's remarkable skill as a performer, noting that her techniques offered insight into suggestion, sensory discrimination, and audience influence.
