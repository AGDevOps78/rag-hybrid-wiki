Classical CAH is associated with higher risk of gestational diabetes.[61][70] Those with classic CAH are at a higher risk of needing to deliver via cesarean section if they have previously undergone vaginoplasty.[61][70] Those with CAH caused by 11-beta-hydroxylase deficiency often experience hypertension; it is advised that they receive care from cardiology specialists during pregnancy.[61] Sixty percent of women with Turner syndrome who become pregnant via egg donation experience complications such as pre-eclampsia, preterm birth, and intrauterine growth restriction.[71] There is also a risk of death for the mother due to root aortic dilation, or the ballooning of the large artery sending blood from the heart.[71][72] This can result in aortic dissection, a splitting of the aorta's walls that causes blood to leak.[71][72] Doctors recommend that women with Turner syndrome be informed of this risk, undergo cardiac review prior to pregnancy, and receive care from a multidisciplinary team while pregnant.[71] Some intersex people are more likely to experience sexual dissatisfaction or difficulty in sexual functioning. For example, 39.3 percent of men with Klinefelter syndrome and 37.1 to 44.1 percent of women with XY DSD are unsatisfied to very unsatisfied with their sex life, compared to 20.8 percent of the general population. This is not universal across all intersex conditions, as those with Turner syndrome and CAH report sex life satisfaction closer to that of the general population.[73] Some intersex conditions are associated with genital dissatisfaction, including concerns about penile length, vaginal length, and clitoral size.[73] Intersex people who have undergone genital surgery may experience reduced sexual sensation and functioning as a result.[74] As of Research suggests intersex patients should be offered psychological support or sex therapy if they express anxiety about sexual function or fear of intimacy.[76] A sexologist can be included in a multidisciplinary team to help address sexual health concerns.[73] Hormone replacement therapy can be used to maintain sexual functioning for some intersex conditions such as ovotesticular DSD.[77] For those with CAIS who have had a gonadectomy, androgen substitution can prove better for sexual well-being and ability to orgasm than oestrogen.[50] Intersex people are at a high risk of developing mental health disorders. A review of 18 studies found that intersex people were more likely to have depressive and anxiety disorders.[78] A survey of intersex adults in the U.S. found that 61.1% of respondents reported having depressive disorders compared to 19% of the general adult population.
