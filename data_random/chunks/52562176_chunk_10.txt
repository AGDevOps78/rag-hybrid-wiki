In China, Zhang Heng (78–139 AD) improved a water clock and invented a seismometer. Ma Jun (200–265 AD) invented a chariot with differential gears. Leo the Philosopher is noted to have worked on a signal system using clocks in the Byzantine Empire in 850, connecting Constantinople with the Cilician Frontier and was a continuation of the complex city clocks in Eastern Rome. These grand machines diffused into the Arabian Empire under Harun al-Rashid. Another grand mechanical device was the Organ, which was reintroduced in 757 when Constantine V gifted one to Pepin the short. With the exception of a few machines, engineering and science stagnated in the West due to the collapse of the Roman Empire during late antiquity.
