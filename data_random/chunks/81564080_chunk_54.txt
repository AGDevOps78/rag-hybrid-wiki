Renaming it allowed the periodical to broaden its scope and become the unofficial organ for all the expanding schools of Suggestive Therapeutics being opened by Dr. Parkyn and his graduates across the country. To support rapid expansion and counter the spread of sectarian systems such as Christian Science, both Sydney B. Flower and W. J. Chatterton were sent to selected cities to organize local institutions and put trained graduates in place as instructors. Chatterton, worked at the Chicago School and had previously conducted extensive experimental work with Thomson Jay Hudson and Prof. William James of Harvard, played a key role in this effort.
