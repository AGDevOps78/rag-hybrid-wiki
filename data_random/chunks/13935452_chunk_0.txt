The circuit of culture is a theory or framework used in the area of cultural studies. The theory was devised in Gerard Goggin openly uses this framework in his book Cell Phone Culture: Mobile technology in everyday life in order to fully understand the cell phone as a cultural artifact. His book is split into four parts: production, consumption, regulation, and representation and identity (through looking at mobile convergences). This culture-related article is a stub.
