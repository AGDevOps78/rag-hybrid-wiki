While studies have shown than musical escapism can be beneficial to mental health, it is important to note its limitations. Music may play a role in mood regulation and enhance positive emotions but its effects are short-lived. Individuals with mental health conditions such as generalized anxiety disorder or post-traumatic stress disorder should acknowledge that the use of music to escape provides temporary relief and is not a substitute for professional help. Music therapy and other types of mental health treatment are recommended to be taken through an experienced counselor with background and training in the field.
