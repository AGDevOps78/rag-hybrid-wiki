If the widow's birth family wanted her to marry again, they would often have to ransom her back from her deceased husband's family. According to Hindu tradition, marrying was a holy act, and an unmarried person was considered impious. In some cases, unmarried people were ineligible to participate in religious ceremonies. It was thought that one's daughter is only temporary, and that she was meant to be her husband's. The main duty of a girl's parents was to arrange her marriage and provide a dowry (an insufficient dowry could be seen as an insult). After marriage, a woman became part of her husband's home, and was a guest in her birth family's home. Wives were expected to serve their husbands.
